# Community Code of Conduct

We want Agent Engineering HQ to be welcoming to practitioners with different backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives. This policy applies to the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and community interactions connected with the project.

Treat people with respect. Critique ideas and implementation choices without personal attacks. Make room for beginners, communicate constructively and respect privacy and consent.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, spam and publication of someone else's private information are not acceptable. Do not pressure maintainers or organizers to accept a contribution, proposal, commercial offer or event commitment.

Report concerns privately to [events@agentengineering.world](mailto:events@agentengineering.world). Include relevant links and the context needed to understand the concern; avoid public allegations or sharing unnecessary personal information. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

Maintainers and organizers may remove content, ask for changes, restrict participation or ban participants when necessary. Decisions depend on context and impact. The same expectations apply to maintainers and organizers.
