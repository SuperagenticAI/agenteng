# Public participation policy

AgentEng publishes sourced event information and organizer policy. It has no accounts, attendee records, personal agendas, bookmarks, proposal drafting, submissions or organizer inbox. Legacy intake settings cannot enable those features.

Use `agenteng participate` to read published policy and official organizer links. London 2026 has an invited programme and no public CFP. Do not infer an open call for another event unless its official page announces one.

Keep private enquiries and personal details out of public GitHub issues and AI chat. Follow official organizer channels for enquiries; this tool does not send messages or collect registrations.
